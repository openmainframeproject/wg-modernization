# Personas for Mainframe Modernization

Mainframe modernization is a multi-dimensional journey involving various stakeholders across the organization. This document defines the primary personas and their key focus areas during a modernization initiative:

## CTO (Chief Technology Officer)
*Goal: Align modernization with business strategy and drive tangible outcomes.*

- **Key Focus Areas**:
    - **Cost Optimization**: Reducing maintenance and licensing costs associated with legacy mainframe infrastructure.
    - **Risk Management**: Ensuring that modernization activities do not introduce security vulnerabilities or operational risks.
    - **ROI (Return on Investment)**: Demonstrating the business value of modernization through improved agility and customer experience.
    - **Innovation Strategy**: Leveraging modern technologies (cloud, AI, etc.) to drive business growth.

## Enterprise Architect
*Goal: Design a robust and scalable architecture for the future of the organization.*

- **Key Focus Areas**:
    - **System Design**: Designing a hybrid-cloud architecture that integrates legacy mainframe components with modern cloud-native services.
    - **Migration Strategy**: Defining the overall roadmap for modernization, including application refactoring, data migration, and infrastructure changes.
    - **Interoperability**: Ensuring seamless communication and data exchange between disparate systems across the enterprise.
    - **Standards and Governance**: Establishing architectural standards and governance processes to ensure consistency and quality.

## Developer
*Goal: Build and maintain the applications and services that power the organization.*

- **Key Focus Areas**:
    - **Tools and Technologies**: Learning and adopting modern development tools, frameworks, and programming languages (e.g., Java, Python, Go).
    - **API Development**: Creating and consuming RESTful APIs to integrate mainframe logic with modern applications.
    - **Modernization Techniques**: Applying modernization techniques such as refactoring, re-platforming, and containerization.
    - **Continuous Integration/Continuous Deployment (CI/CD)**: Implementing automated CI/CD pipelines to accelerate software delivery and improve quality.
    
## Operations / Platform Engineer
*Goal: Ensure reliability, availability, and operational efficiency of modernized systems.*

- **Key Focus Areas**:
    - System monitoring and observability
    - Deployment automation and reliability
    - Incident response and system resilience
---
*Note: These personas are intended to provide a starting point for understanding the diverse perspectives and requirements in a mainframe modernization project.*
